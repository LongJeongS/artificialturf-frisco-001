About the Area

About The Colony

The Colony runs along the eastern shore of Lewisville Lake in Denton County, a short drive southwest of Frisco. Being near the water doesn't do much for a lawn in August, and the same heat and slow-draining clay that shape yards in Frisco shape them here.

Many backyards in The Colony are fenced suburban lots with a patio and a stretch of grass that gets the most foot traffic. That's usually where turf earns its keep, along with dog runs and the strip beside a pool. Many people start there.

We build on compacted crushed stone sloped toward a low point, so rain has a way out instead of sitting on the clay, and we pick the turf grade and infill for how the space gets used, whether that's dogs, kids or a few putts.

If part of your yard in The Colony keeps going to mud or dust, tell us about the space and we'll follow up with a written quote.
